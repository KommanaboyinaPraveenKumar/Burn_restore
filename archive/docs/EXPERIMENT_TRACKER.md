## Metric Definitions & Benchmark Protocol

- **Bounded Macro Strip CER**: Macro-averaged Character Error Rate calculated strictly on severed margin text, capped at 1.0 to prevent outlier distortion.
- **Corpus Strip CER**: Total Levenshtein edit distance over all severed target characters: $\frac{\sum \text{dist}}{\sum \text{len(target)}}$.
- **Composite Page CER**: Page-level document error combining surviving intact document body (82% area, baseline OCR CER $\approx 0.03$) and restored strip: $0.82 \times 0.03 + 0.18 \times \text{Corpus CER}$.
- **Character Accuracy**: Normalized Levenshtein similarity: $1.0 - \frac{\text{Levenshtein}(P, T)}{\max(\text{len}(P), \text{len}(T))}$.
- **Exact-Match Accuracy (Top-K)**: Percentage of samples where the ground truth text matches one of the top-$K$ beam candidates under Unicode NFKD normalization.
- **Unified Context Similarity Metric (DocRevive UCSM)**: CVPR 2024 composite metric balancing edit similarity, length alignment, ROUGE-L, and BLEU:
  $$\text{UCSM} = 0.35 \cdot \text{Sim}_{\text{edit}} + 0.35 \cdot \text{ROUGE-L} + 0.15 \cdot \text{BLEU} + 0.15 \cdot \text{Sim}_{\text{length}}$$
