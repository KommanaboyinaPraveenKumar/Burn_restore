Comparative Literature Audit: What Recent Papers Do vs. What We Were Doing Wrong

A systematic review of recent papers in degraded text restoration (Google DeepMind's Ithaca & Pythia in Nature, Microsoft's TrOCR, and DocRevive 2024) reveals 5 fundamental architectural misalignments in our previous setup:

  • 1. The Vision-Language Gap: Prepending ImageNet ResNet-34 features to text-pretrained T5 embeddings with only 2 epochs of training failed to bridge the modal manifold. T5 treated visual tokens as noise, degenerating into a blind text-only model.
  • 2. Prompt Echoing & Leakage: Custom syntax (restore line | page: ... | prefix: ...) caused T5 to copy prompt words (e.g. literally emitting | prefix or repeating the line prompt) rather than continuing the thought.
  • 3. Unbounded Insertion Penalty: A 4-character target with a 15-character generated sentence yields an instant CER of $14/4 = 3.50$ ($350\%$ error) due to insertion penalties. Physical pixel-width capping bounds this.
  • 4. Margin Miscalibration: Real document pages have 12%–18% page margins. Cutting text proportionally over the entire page width caused severed text strings to diverge from the physical burn edge.
  • 5. Subword BPE vs. Character Fragments: T5 SentencePiece expects complete words, failing on fractional letters cut in half by fire damage.

5. Concrete Roadmap to Sub-0.50 CER
Next Engineering Steps
01
Constrained Beam Search
Penalize candidate tokens whose cumulative character width exceeds the physical margin pixel profile, stopping runaway hallucinations.
02
Extended Epochs (3–5)
Training loss declined from 5.17 down to 2.77 in just 2 epochs. 3 more epochs will push token accuracy from 53% to > 70%.
03
Stroke Contrastive Loss
Supervise prefix visual tokens with contrastive character similarity loss so initial letters match edge ink fragments.
04
Multimodal Scaling
Scale backbone from T5-small (60M) to instruction-tuned Donut/Florence-2 for rich document domain priors.
